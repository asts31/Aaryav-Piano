How lessons work

What to expect

A structure built for steady, visible progress — whether the goal is a first recital or an RCM exam.

Every student starts with a short intro session to figure out where they're at and what they want out of piano — whether that's just having fun at the keys, working toward an RCM exam, or preparing for a recital.

  1. Intro session

    A quick chat and short playing assessment to set a starting point and goals.

  2. Weekly lessons

    30–45 minute one-on-one sessions focused on technique, reading, and repertoire.

  3. Practice plans

    Clear, simple practice goals between lessons — no guessing what to work on.

  4. Recitals & exams

    Performance and RCM exam prep when a student is ready for it.
